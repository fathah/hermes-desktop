# Master Service Agreement

## 1. Parties and Term

This Agreement is between the Contractor (the security provider) and BlueBay
Retail Park Ltd (the Client). The initial term is 24 months from the commencement
date, renewing for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives 90 days'
written notice. Site-specific duties are defined in the [[post-orders-bluebay]],
which form part of this Agreement.

## 2. Scope of Services

The Contractor provides manned guarding, mobile patrol, and key-holding for the
retail park. Coverage is 24 hours, 365 days. Minimum staffing is two officers on
days and one officer plus one mobile patrol on nights. Officers operate to the
[[field-operations-sop]] and are graded against the [[incident-response-matrix]].

## 3. Service Levels

The Contractor shall maintain the following service levels. Roster fill: a minimum
of 98% of contracted hours delivered each month. Response: on a Code Red alarm
activation the on-site officer attends within 3 minutes and the mobile patrol
within 20 minutes. Reporting: the Daily Occurrence Book is shared with the Client
each morning by 09:00, and a monthly KPI report is delivered within five working
days of month end.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators and Service Credits

Performance is measured monthly. Where a service level is missed, service credits
(financial penalties netted against the invoice) apply. The schedule is:

- Roster fill below 98% but at or above 95%: 2% of the monthly charge.
- Roster fill below 95%: 5% of the monthly charge.
- Each missed or falsified patrol: a service credit of £150 per occurrence.
- Each late monthly KPI report: £250.
- A Code Red response-time breach: £1,000 per breach.

Service credits are capped at 20% of the monthly charge. Persistent breach — three
consecutive months at the 5% level — is a material breach entitling the Client to
terminate on 30 days' notice.

## 5. Charges and Invoicing

Charges are at the agreed hourly rate per officer-hour, invoiced monthly in
arrears. Payment terms are 30 days from a valid invoice. Annual rate review is
linked to the published National Living Wage uplift and is applied each April.

## 6. Insurance and Liability

The Contractor maintains public liability cover of £10 million and employer's
liability of £10 million. The Contractor's aggregate liability under this
Agreement is capped at the total charges paid in the preceding 12 months, except
for liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 7. Data and Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's confidential information secure. CCTV and personal
data are handled under the Contractor's data-protection policy and applicable law.
