# Training Matrix

## 1. Mandatory Training

All permanent staff hold a valid SIA licence and complete induction training
before their first shift. Mandatory courses are refreshed on a fixed cycle: first
aid every three years, fire marshal every two years, and conflict management
yearly. A member of staff whose certification lapses is stood down until it is
renewed.

## 2. Yearly Refresher

Every member of staff attends a yearly refresher covering assignment instructions,
data protection, and health and safety updates. Attendance is recorded against the
individual on the training register, and the register is audited each quarter.

## 3. Development

Staff may request additional development — supervisory, CCTV operator, or close
protection courses — through their line manager. Approved courses are funded by
the company and counted as paid working time. Completed qualifications are added to
the staff member's record.
