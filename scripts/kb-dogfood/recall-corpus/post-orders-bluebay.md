# Post Orders — BlueBay Retail Park

These site instructions sit under the [[master-service-agreement]] and the
[[field-operations-sop]]. Where these post orders are silent, the SOP applies.

## 1. Site Layout and Keys

The retail park has three vehicle entrances (North, Service, and Customer) and a
central management suite. The night officer holds the master key bunch; the
service gate is locked from 20:00 and reopened at 06:00 for deliveries.

## 2. Patrol Requirements

The mobile patrol completes a full external circuit of all units twice per night,
logged at the NFC checkpoints. A missed checkpoint scan counts as a missed patrol
and is penalised under the service-credit schedule in the
[[master-service-agreement]]; officers must therefore never skip a checkpoint even
when responding to an alarm — call control to arrange cover instead.

## 3. Medical Emergencies

For any medical emergency on site, do not improvise the call order. Follow the
escalation chain exactly as set out in the [[incident-response-matrix]], which
lists who is contacted first and in what order. The defibrillator is mounted
outside the management suite; the nearest A&E is St Mary's, four miles north.

## 4. Loss Prevention

Suspected shoplifting is handled by store staff, not the security officer. The
officer supports only when asked and when it is safe, and never detains a person
off the store's instruction. All incidents are graded under the
[[incident-response-matrix]] and logged.

## 5. Severe Weather

On a flood or storm warning the officer checks the service-yard drains, moves the
patrol vehicle to higher ground in the multi-storey, and reports closures to
control.
