# Payroll Policy

## 1. Pay Runs

Permanent staff are paid monthly by bank transfer on the last working day. New
starters joining mid-month are paid a pro-rated amount. Payslips are issued
electronically and show gross pay, deductions, and net pay.

## 2. Allowances

Several allowances are payable on top of basic pay. A shift allowance applies to
nights and weekends. A boot-and-equipment allowance is paid annually each April.
A standby allowance applies when a member of staff is rostered on call. All
allowances are taxable and shown separately on the payslip.

## 3. Deductions and Statutory Pay

Statutory deductions (income tax, National Insurance, pension auto-enrolment) are
applied at source. Statutory sick pay and statutory maternity/paternity pay follow
the published statutory schemes. Court-ordered deductions are actioned on receipt
of a valid order.

## 4. Annual Review

Pay rates for permanent staff are reviewed annually, with any uplift effective
from April and linked to the National Living Wage. Vacation pay accrual, overtime,
and allowances are all recalculated at the review. Queries about an individual's
pay or allowances go to the Finance Manager.
