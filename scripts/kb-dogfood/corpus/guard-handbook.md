# Guard Handbook

## 1. Welcome

This handbook sets out the employment terms, conduct standards, and entitlements
for all guarding personnel. It complements the operational rules in the
[[field-operations-sop]]. Where this handbook covers conduct, it is the
authority; where it covers a procedure on shift, the SOP governs.

## 2. Uniform and Equipment

Every officer is issued a uniform and must present it clean and complete. The
standard day-shift uniform is black trousers, a white shirt with epaulettes, a
clip-on tie, and the company softshell jacket. Night-shift and external postings
add a hi-visibility coat and a beanie in cold weather. Footwear is plain black
safety boots. The SIA licence is worn on the upper arm at all times. Lost
equipment is reported and may be recharged to the officer if negligence is found.

## 3. Grooming and Presentation

Personnel represent the company and the client, so presentation matters.
Hair is kept tidy and off the collar. Where a beard is worn it must be neatly
trimmed to no more than 15 millimetres; stubble is not acceptable on a client
site. Visible tattoos on the face or neck must be covered. Jewellery is limited
to a wedding band and a single pair of plain studs. Strong fragrance is avoided
on concierge postings.

## 4. Hours, Breaks, and Pay

Full-time officers work an average 42-hour week over a rolling four-week roster.
A shift over six hours carries a 30-minute unpaid break. Pay is monthly, on the
last working day, by bank transfer. Overtime beyond the contracted average is
paid at 1.25 times the basic rate, and bank-holiday shifts are paid at 1.5 times.
Wage queries go to the HR Manager, not the control room.

## 5. Holiday Entitlement

Full-time officers receive a holiday entitlement of 28 working days per annum,
inclusive of public holidays, accruing monthly from the start date. Holiday is
requested through the roster system at least 14 days in advance and is subject to
operational cover. Untaken entitlement does not roll over beyond two days into
the next leave year.

## 6. Conduct and Discipline

The company operates a staged disciplinary process: informal counselling, first
written warning, final written warning, and dismissal. Certain matters are gross
misconduct and may lead to dismissal without notice — these include sleeping on
duty, leaving a post unmanned, intoxication, dishonesty, and any failure to meet
the Code Red timeline defined in the [[field-operations-sop]]. An officer may be
suspended on full pay pending investigation.

## 7. Separation and Notice

When an officer chooses to leave, the required notice depends on length of
service. An officer who has completed the 12-week probation must give a minimum
of four weeks' written notice of resignation; during probation the notice is one
week. The company matches these periods when initiating a separation, except in a
gross-misconduct dismissal. On the final day the officer returns all uniform, the
SIA licence holder, keys, and any company device. The final salary, including
accrued untaken holiday, is paid in the next normal pay run.
